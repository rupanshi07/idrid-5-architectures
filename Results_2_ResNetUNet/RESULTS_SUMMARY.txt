ARCHITECTURE 2: U-Net + ResNet34 Backbone (pretrained)
================================================================================
Preprocessing: CLAHE applied to every image (train/val/test)
Segmentation: EX + SE (exudates only)
Training: 40 epochs, ImageNet-pretrained encoder, GPU (RTX 2050)

Per-lesion Dice: EX 0.7692, SE 0.6422
Mean Dice: 0.7057
Accuracy: 0.5437
Quadratic Kappa: 0.6637

Comparison vs Architecture 1 (no pretraining): +0.083 Dice, +0.189 Kappa --
demonstrates clear benefit of transfer learning on this small dataset.
