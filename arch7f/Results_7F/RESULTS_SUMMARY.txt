ARCHITECTURE 7F: Full Faculty Specification (Bottleneck Transformer + Soft
Segmentation-Guided Fusion + Dedicated Classification Transformer with

Mean Dice: 0.6947 (EX 0.7239, SE 0.6654)

FULL PROGRESSION (faculty-guided chain):
  7A       Dice 0.6941  Acc 0.4272  Kappa 0.7428
  7A-v3    Dice 0.6981  Acc 0.6214  Kappa 0.7185
  7E       Dice 0.7065  Acc 0.6602  Kappa 0.6483
  7F       Dice 0.6947  Acc 0.4757  Kappa 0.6681

FINDING: 7F combined six new components simultaneously (soft mask fusion,
dedicated classification transformer, attention pooling, Focal-Tversky
loss, hybrid CE+ordinal loss, class weighting) rather than isolating one
variable at a time, unlike the successful 7E step. Result: Kappa
partially recovered vs 7E (+0.02) but did NOT reach the 0.72-0.75+
target, while accuracy and Dice both regressed from 7E's levels. This
does not meet the specific success condition (recover Kappa while
keeping 7E's Dice/accuracy gains).

No single model in this faculty-guided line dominates on all three
metrics: 7A has best Kappa (0.7428), 7E has best Dice (0.7065) and
best accuracy (0.6602). This suggests these three objectives may be in
genuine tension for this architecture family, dataset size, and
training budget -- consistent with the faculty''s own observation that
"90% Dice and 80% accuracy should be treated as targets rather than
guaranteed outcomes."

LESSON: Combining multiple simultaneous architectural/loss changes
(as in 7F and earlier 7D) makes it impossible to attribute results to
specific components, unlike the clean, isolated 7E experiment. Future
work should isolate one variable at a time, as the faculty explicitly
recommended after 7D''s collapse.
