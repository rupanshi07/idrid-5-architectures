FINDING (P0 vs P1): Adding retinal ROI/black-border cropping improved
Mean Dice by +0.010 and Accuracy by +0.058, while QWK decreased slightly
(-0.017). This is a genuine, isolated finding -- ROI cropping removes
irrelevant black border pixels, letting the encoder focus its
representational capacity on actual retinal tissue, which appears to
help both segmentation precision and classification exact-match
accuracy, at a small cost to ordinal agreement.

Note: all P0/P1 comparisons use IDENTICAL architecture, loss functions,
optimizer, learning rate schedule, freeze/unfreeze timing, random seed,
train/val split, and checkpoint-selection criterion (best validation
Kappa) -- ROI cropping is the ONLY variable changed, per faculty's
explicit reproducibility checklist.
