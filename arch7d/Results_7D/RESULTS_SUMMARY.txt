ARCHITECTURE 7D: Shared Bottleneck Transformer + Segmentation-Guided Classifier
================================================================================
Mean Dice: 0.6965 (EX 0.7301, SE 0.6630) -- solid, comparable to other Swin variants
Accuracy: 0.2816   Quadratic Kappa: 0.3688 (COLLAPSED)

FINDING: This is the THIRD architecture in this project where a shared or
heavily-coupled Transformer bottleneck caused classification to collapse
while segmentation remained solid (following 7A-Ordinal-v1 and v2). The
design made the bottleneck Transformer serve both segmentation (needs
fine spatial detail) and classification (needs global summary)
simultaneously -- this appears to create a harder, competing optimization
problem than keeping the two pathways more separate, as in the
successful 7A and 7A-v3 designs. This is a well-evidenced, replicated
finding: shared bottleneck representations for joint seg+cls tasks are
harder to train successfully than task-specific branches, at least
under this dataset size and compute budget.

Best faculty-guided result remains 7A v3 (Dice 0.6981, Acc 0.6214, Kappa 0.7185).
Best overall project result remains the GAN+7A ensemble (Dice 0.7098, Kappa 0.7730).
