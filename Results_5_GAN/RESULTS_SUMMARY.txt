ARCHITECTURE 5: New Innovation -- Adversarial Segmentation Refinement (GAN)
================================================================================
Generator: Architecture 3B (ResNet34 + SE blocks + Attention Gates)
Discriminator: 6-layer PatchGAN, judges (image, mask) pairs as real/fake
Training: Adam for both G and D, adversarial weight 0.1, full precision (no AMP)

Per-lesion Dice: EX 0.7362, SE 0.6084
Mean Dice: 0.6723
Accuracy: 0.5922
Quadratic Kappa: 0.7557  (BEST classification result across all 5 architectures)

FINDING: Adding an adversarial loss on top of the best-performing generator
(Architecture 3B) improved classification Kappa further (0.7238 -> 0.7557)
and notably improved grade-4 (severe DR) detection, the most clinically
important class. Segmentation Dice was strong but slightly below 3B alone
(0.6723 vs 0.7044), suggesting the adversarial objective traded a small
amount of raw pixel-overlap accuracy for better overall structural/
classification coherence.
