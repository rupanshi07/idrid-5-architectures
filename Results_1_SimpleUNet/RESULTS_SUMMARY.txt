ARCHITECTURE 1: Simple U-Net Multiclass (baseline, no pretraining, no attention)
================================================================================
Preprocessing: CLAHE applied to every image (train/val/test)
Segmentation: EX + SE (exudates only)
Training: 40 epochs, from scratch, GPU (RTX 2050)

Per-lesion Dice: EX 0.6934, SE 0.5511
Mean Dice: 0.6223
Accuracy: 0.4563
Quadratic Kappa: 0.4747

Note: Model never predicted grade 1 on any test sample -- weakest class,
likely due to no pretraining + limited epochs for the rarest DR grade.
