ARCHITECTURE 7B: Swin Transformer + SE + Attention Gates + Segmentation-Guided Classifier
================================================================================
Mean Dice: 0.6745 (EX 0.7254, SE 0.6236)
Accuracy: 0.4660   Quadratic Kappa: 0.7193

FINDING: Using the predicted exudate mask to guide classification gave a
solid, stable result but did not outperform 7A (plain Swin+Attention,
Kappa 0.7428) or Architecture 5 (GAN, Kappa 0.7557). The segmentation-
guidance idea is sound but did not provide additional benefit here,
likely because Swin''s SE-attended features already capture most of
the relevant lesion information implicitly.
