FINAL MODEL: Ensemble of Architecture 5 (GAN) + Architecture 7A (Swin + Attention)
================================================================================
Method: Average sigmoid probabilities for segmentation, average softmax
probabilities for classification, across both models' predictions.
No retraining required -- pure inference-time combination.

Per-lesion Dice: EX 0.7625, SE 0.6571
Mean Dice: 0.7098  (best segmentation result across ALL architectures)
Accuracy: 0.4854
Quadratic Kappa: 0.7730  (best classification agreement across ALL architectures)

CONCLUSION: After testing 9+ individual architectures (simple U-Net, ResNet
backbone, attention mechanisms, custom transformer bottleneck, TransUNet,
GAN-based adversarial refinement, and Swin Transformer variants), each
landing in a similar 0.55-0.71 Dice / 0.26-0.76 Kappa range depending on
design, ensembling the two best-performing, most complementary models
(GAN and Swin+Attention) produced the best overall result. This
demonstrates that model combination, not further single-architecture
tuning, was the most effective final lever for this dataset and compute
budget.
