ARCHITECTURE 4: TransUNet (Hybrid CNN + Transformer Bottleneck, no pretraining)
================================================================================
Mean Dice: 0.5932 (EX 0.7310, SE 0.4554)
Accuracy: 0.2913   Quadratic Kappa: 0.4734

FINDING: Segmentation performance is reasonable (between Arch 1 and Arch 2),
but classification accuracy collapsed to near-random (29%). Likely cause:
Vision Transformers are known to be data-hungry and typically require
large-scale pretraining (e.g. ImageNet-21k) to learn useful global
representations -- with only 680 training images and no pretraining, the
transformer bottleneck could not learn a discriminative representation
for 5-class DR grading. This matches well-documented ViT literature on
small-dataset performance without pretraining.
